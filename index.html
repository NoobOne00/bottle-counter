Detection zone

Drag on the picture to draw a narrow box across the bottles' path, a little taller than the tallest bottle. Each bottle must pass fully through it, with belt visible between bottles.

Tips: fix the phone on a tripod or clamp — don't hand-hold. Use side-on view. Avoid people walking through the zone. Keep the lighting steady.

Teach the AI

Hold a button to capture samples (about 8 per second). Aim for 30+ each. Capture BOTTLE only while a bottle is fully inside the zone, and EMPTY only when the zone shows bare belt.

Check: watch the confidence bar above as bottles pass — it should rise above the right marker for every bottle and drop below the left marker between them. If not, add more samples of the case it gets wrong.

Saved line setups

Saves the zone and the taught samples so you can reuse them on the same line with the phone in the same position.
